# 02_KnowledgeVault

## Purpose

The intellectual memory of ResearchAI. This folder is designed to be opened as an Obsidian vault and serves as the structured knowledge backbone of the entire research system.

## What Goes Here

- Paper reading notes (linked to papers in `01_Literature/`)
- Concept definitions and method descriptions
- Dataset summaries and characteristics
- Research questions and hypotheses
- Experiment interpretations and result analyses
- Cross-cutting topic maps and literature matrices

## Obsidian Usage

This folder should be opened as an Obsidian vault. Key conventions to adopt:

- Use `[[wikilinks]]` for internal knowledge connections
- Use tags for topic classification
- Use templates for consistent note structure
- Maintain a MOC (Map of Content) for navigation

## Relationship to Other Directories

- Receives processed knowledge from `01_Literature/`
- Informs research direction in `07_Research_Ideas/`
- Provides context for experiments in `05_Experiments/`
- Supplies material for writing in `06_Writing/`
- Guides project decisions in `03_Projects/`
