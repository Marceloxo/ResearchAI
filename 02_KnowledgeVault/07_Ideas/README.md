# 07_Ideas — Research Ideas and Hypotheses

## Purpose

A living collection of research ideas, hypotheses, and conjectures. This is the creative, forward-looking layer of the vault — where gaps become questions and questions become proposals.

## Contents

- Research idea notes (e.g., `Idea - Multi-Scale Attention for Fault Detection`).
- Hypothesis statements with testable predictions.
- Gap analysis notes derived from literature surveys.
- Brainstorming sessions and mind maps.
- Prioritization notes (feasibility, impact, novelty).

## Relationship to Other Directories

- Emerges from gaps identified in `01_Papers/` and `02_Topics/`.
- Grounded in methods from `03_Methods/` and tasks from `04_Tasks/`.
- References datasets in `05_Datasets/` for feasibility assessment.
- Tested via experiments in `06_Experiments/`.
- Mature ideas graduate to projects in `03_Projects/` (external to vault).
- Feeds into writing in `08_Writing/`.

## AI Agent Usage

1. When analyzing a set of papers, proactively generate gap notes here.
2. Tag ideas by feasibility and impact for prioritization.
3. Link each idea to the specific paper gap or method limitation that inspired it.
4. Periodically review and promote mature ideas to project status.
