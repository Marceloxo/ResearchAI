# Research Map / 研究地图

This is the highest-level map of ResearchAI's research scope. It shows what we work on, what we might work on, and how different areas connect.

---

## Current Focus / 当前重点

### Seismic AI

Primary research direction: applying deep learning and computer vision to seismic data processing and interpretation.

- **Entry Point**: [[Seismic_AI_Map]]

---

## Core Technologies / 核心技术

These are the foundational technologies that underpin all research directions.

### Deep Learning

Neural network architectures, training strategies, and optimization methods.

- **Entry Point**: [[Deep_Learning_Map]]

### Computer Vision

Image processing, segmentation, detection, and generation techniques.

- **Entry Point**: [[Deep_Learning_Map]] (CV methods are embedded in DL methods)

---

## Future Extensions / 未来扩展

Research areas that may be explored in future stages. No active work yet.

### Medical Imaging

- Applications: CT/MRI analysis, lesion detection, organ segmentation
- Relationship to current work: shares methods (CNN, Transformer, segmentation) with seismic AI

### Remote Sensing

- Applications: satellite image analysis, land cover classification, change detection
- Relationship to current work: similar data characteristics (large-scale, multi-channel, geospatial)

### Other Vision Tasks

- Applications: industrial inspection, video understanding, 3D reconstruction
- Relationship to current work: transferable CV/ML methodology

---

## Cross-Cutting Themes / 跨领域主题

These themes span multiple research areas:

- **Limited Labeled Data**: semi-supervised learning, self-supervised learning, transfer learning
- **Multi-Scale Analysis**: handling features at different spatial/temporal scales
- **Domain Adaptation**: transferring models across different data distributions
- **Interpretable AI**: understanding why models make predictions
