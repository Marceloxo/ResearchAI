# 02_Topics — Research Topic Overviews

## Purpose

High-level overview notes for research topics and subfields. Topic notes aggregate knowledge across papers, methods, tasks, and experiments to paint a coherent picture of a research area.

## Contents

- Topic overview notes (e.g., `Topic - Seismic Fault Detection`).
- Literature surveys and mini-reviews organized by topic.
- Comparison tables of methods within a topic.
- Open problems and research frontier summaries.

## Relationship to Other Directories

- Aggregates paper notes from `01_Papers/`.
- Links to specific methods in `03_Methods/`.
- Links to relevant tasks in `04_Tasks/`.
- References datasets in `05_Datasets/`.
- Identifies gaps that feed into `07_Ideas/`.
- Serves as entry points indexed from `00_Meta/` MOC files.

## AI Agent Usage

1. Use topic notes as the starting point when asked "what is the state of research on X?".
2. When a topic note grows too large, split it into sub-topic notes and update the MOC.
3. Regularly update topic notes with findings from newly read papers.
4. Generate comparison tables automatically when multiple methods address the same task.
