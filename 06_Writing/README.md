# 06_Writing

## Purpose

Scientific writing — the output layer of the research pipeline.

## What Goes Here

- Literature reviews
- Research proposals
- Paper drafts and manuscripts
- Conference and journal submissions
- Rebuttal letters and revisions

## Suggested Structure

Each writing project should have its own subdirectory with:

- The main manuscript (LaTeX or Markdown)
- Figures and tables
- Reference bibliography
- Revision history

## Relationship to Other Directories

- Draws on knowledge from `02_KnowledgeVault/`
- Cites papers from `01_Literature/`
- Reports results from `05_Experiments/`
- Develops ideas from `07_Research_Ideas/`
