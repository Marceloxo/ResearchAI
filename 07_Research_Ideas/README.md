# 07_Research_Ideas

## Purpose

Research idea management — a space for exploring, recording, and developing research directions.

## What Goes Here

- Potential research topics
- Identified research gaps
- Hypotheses and conjectures
- Future research directions
- Brainstorming notes and mind maps

## Guidelines

- Ideas should be linked to relevant literature in `01_Literature/`
- Promising ideas should reference knowledge in `02_KnowledgeVault/`
- Mature ideas should graduate into `03_Projects/`
- Keep ideas atomic — one file per concept for easy linking

## Relationship to Other Directories

- Informed by literature gaps found in `01_Literature/` and `02_KnowledgeVault/`
- Seeds new projects in `03_Projects/`
- Drives experiment design in `05_Experiments/`
- Provides direction for writing in `06_Writing/`
